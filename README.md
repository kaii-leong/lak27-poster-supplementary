## Table 1: Finalised Codebook

| Codename | Definition | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ABILITY | Expressions of confidence or demonstrations of a student's technical, critical, communication, or academic competencies. | communication skill, academic writing ability, quality of final arguments structure |
| ANALYSIS INACCURACIES | Concerns that raw data could lead to misinterpretation, false assumptions, or incorrect conclusions by assessors. | I would rather not share this with teacher as this could create biases |
| CONTRIBUTION | Rationales focused on demonstrating authentic individual effort, authorship, and ownership of the work. | Teachers can assess my work and know how much work is done by me |
| DEFICIENCIES | Exposing personal struggles, mistakes, low proficiency, or hesitations during the learning process. | it reveals full writing process and mistakes |
| FINAL PRODUCT | Data required solely because it forms part of the final deliverable. | It's the finalised form of the assessment |
| IMPROVEMENT | Rationales focused on utilising data to drive learning growth, refine skills, enhance future performance, or guide pedagogical support | Teachers can help me identify areas of improvement. |
| LEARNING PROCESS | Rationales focused on exposing the student's cognitive workflow, research strategy, and problem-solving approach. | it might show ur developement throughout the process |
| PEER PRESSURE (HORIZONTAL) | Concerns regarding social comparison, judgement, or perceived inadequacy when student data or metrics are exposed to classmates. | Not every student can finish a report in the same time |
| PLAGIARISM | Concerns regarding unauthorised copying, intellectual property theft, or uncredited use of work by peers. | keep privacy from other student to not copy |
| PRESSURE (VERTICAL) | Feelings of anxiety or discomfort from being observed, monitored, or evaluated by authoritative figures. | I don't want any pressure of someone checking |
| PRIVACY | Concerns regarding personal boundaries, surveillance anxiety, or unauthorised exposure of personal information. | Would not share with anyone as could be considered personal data |
| PROTECTION | Rationales focused on using evidence to defend against academic integrity allegations, prove genuine authorship, or safeguard against false claims | Needed in the case of plagiarism cases |
| REDUNDANCY | Data perceived as unnecessary, noisy, or having low value for evaluating the task. | I don't think it'd be so important for teachers |
| SCOPE | Rationales that place conditions on data sharing based on boundaries, platform restrictions, or the level of detail/aggregation. | Should be fine if it's on a confined platform |
